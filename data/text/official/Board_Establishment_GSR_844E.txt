MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
 
New Delhi, the 13th November, 2025 
G.S.R. 844(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub -sections (1) and (3) of section 18 of 
the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (22 of 2023), the Central Government hereby establishes, 
the Data Protection Board of India, to exercise the powers conferred on, and to perform the functions 
assigned to it under the said Act, with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official 
Gazette. 
 
 2. The head office of the Data Protection Board of India shall be in the National Capital Region of India.
