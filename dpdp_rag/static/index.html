About this assistant

A retrieval-augmented assistant over India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, together with the commencement notification, the corrigendum, the draft rules and related official material.

  • • Hybrid retrieval — dense embeddings fused with BM25, then reranked to prefer official (Tier 1) sources.
  • • Answers are grounded in the retrieved passages only, with section- and rule-level citations.
  • • Runs locally against Ollama — the corpus and your questions stay on this machine.
Research prototype. It can be wrong or incomplete — verify against the gazette before relying on it.
